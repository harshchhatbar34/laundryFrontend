# Luxury Laundry Design Prompt

## Role

You are a Principal Product Designer, UX Architect, Motion Designer,
Brand Designer and Creative Director.

Redesign my Laundry Management application.

### Rules

-   Do NOT change business logic.
-   Do NOT change APIs.
-   Do NOT change workflows.
-   Do NOT change navigation.
-   Redesign only UI, UX, branding, colors, typography, spacing, icons,
    illustrations and motion.

## Brand

Premium, trustworthy, elegant, modern, luxurious, clean.

## Color Palette

-   Deep Navy: #0B1E3B
-   Solar Gold: #C9A227
-   Electric Blue: #2563EB
-   Trust Teal: #1F8A80
-   White: #FFFFFF
-   Background: #F7F9FC

Use Deep Navy for navigation and headers. Use Solar Gold for highlights,
KPI icons and premium accents. Use Electric Blue for interactive
elements and charts. Use Trust Teal for status and service indicators.
Use White for cards and surfaces.

## Visual Style

Rounded corners (20-24px), floating cards, soft shadows, premium
typography, spacious layouts, minimal borders.

## Components

Redesign buttons, cards, inputs, navigation, dialogs, bottom sheets,
charts, tables, snackbars, progress indicators, empty states and loading
states.

## Dashboard

Premium KPI cards with expressive icons, animated counters, trend
indicators, sparklines and soft gradients.

## Icons

Create custom icons with personality. Dashboard: cards wake up. Orders:
basket fills. Customers: avatar waves. Services: soap bubble inflates.
Payments: coin rotates. Reports: chart grows. Settings: gear rotates.

Animate meaningful parts only.

## Motion

Spring physics, anticipation, overshoot, secondary motion, duration
220-300ms.

## Ambient Motion

Cards gently float. Gold highlights shimmer. Icons breathe every 10-15
seconds. Illustrations subtly move. Pause while user interacts.

## Micro Interactions

Buttons lift with gold glow. Cards elevate and scale to 1.02. Navigation
uses smooth active indicator.

## Final Goal

The application should feel like a premium luxury laundry brand inspired
by Apple, Airbnb, Stripe and Linear, consistently using Deep Navy, Solar
Gold, Electric Blue, Trust Teal and White.
